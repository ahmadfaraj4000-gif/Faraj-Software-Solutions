The Challenge of Restaurant Pricing

Most restaurants leave money on the table

Without precise costing, you're either undercharging (losing profit) or overcharging (losing customers). The real challenge? Every menu item has hidden costs that are difficult to track:

  • • Waste — trim, spoilage, over-preparation
  • • Labor — different roles at different wages
  • • Overhead — how much of your rent goes into each burger?
  • • Competitor positioning — where do you fit in the market?

Weighted Labor Tiers

Different roles earn different wages. A manager's time costs more than a dishwasher's. Pricing Assistant calculates the weighted average based on hours per week — so your labor cost reflects reality.

Why it matters: A burger made by a $25/hr manager costs more than one made by an $18/hr cook. Know the difference.
🏢

Overhead Allocation

Track every expense: rent, insurance, utilities, POS fees, marketing — even cleaning supplies. Pricing Assistant allocates a fair share to each menu item based on your monthly volume.

Why it matters: If you sell 3,000 items a month, each one carries a portion of your $6,000 rent. That's $2 per item you might be forgetting.
🗑️

Waste Tracking

Set waste percentages per ingredient (trim, spoilage, over-preparation). Or use global defaults. Pricing Assistant calculates the actual cost of waste per item.

Why it matters: 8% waste on beef isn't just $0.40 — it's profit you'll never see. Factor it in or lose it.
🔄

What-if Scenarios

Test changes before they happen: ingredient price hikes, labor cost increases, overhead changes, volume fluctuations. See the impact on your prices instantly.

Why it matters: Avocado prices spike 15%? Run the scenario and adjust your menu prices before you lose margin.
📊

Competitor Analysis

Track up to 3 competitor prices per item. See the min, max, and average — and how your recommended price compares to the market.

Why it matters: Price too high and customers leave. Price too low and you leave money on the table. Find the sweet spot.
⏱️

Batch Labor Calculator

Enter prep time and batch yield — Pricing Assistant calculates labor minutes per item automatically. No more manual estimates.

Why it matters: 2 hours of prep for 30 burger patties = 4 minutes per patty. Simple math, but easy to forget.

Strategic pricing starts here

🎯 Run discounts without losing money

Know your exact floor price before running any promotion. Pricing Assistant shows you the minimum price that covers all costs — so you can discount confidently without eroding margin.

Example: Happy hour 20% off on burgers. Your true cost is $8.42, so $9.99 keeps you profitable. Anything below $8.42 loses money.

⚖️ Identify loss leaders strategically

Sometimes you need to price below cost to drive traffic. Pricing Assistant shows you exactly how much subsidy you're providing, so you can track whether the cross-sell makes up for it.

Example: Price wings at $6.99 (cost $8.50) — you lose $1.51 per order. But if 80% of wing buyers order drinks ($5 profit), you win.
