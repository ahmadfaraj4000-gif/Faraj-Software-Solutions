Shift Planner FAQ

Common questions from small and medium-sized businesses comparing scheduling tools, labor cost calculators, and weekly shift planners.

What is Shift Planner?

Shift Planner is a web-based employee schedule builder that helps small and medium-sized businesses build weekly staff schedules, estimate labor cost, compare labor against projected revenue, and review coverage before shifts are finalized.

How does Shift Planner help control labor cost?

It uses employee wages, shift times, revenue targets, labor percentage targets, and coverage needs to show whether the schedule is staying inside the business's labor expectations.

Can I save, export, and print schedules?

Yes. Shift Planner includes saved schedules, CSV export, and a print-friendly view so managers can review or share the weekly schedule.

How do customers access Shift Planner?

Customers access Shift Planner through the Faraj Software Solutions client portal after signing in with an active subscription.
