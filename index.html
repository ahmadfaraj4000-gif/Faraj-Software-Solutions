Product FAQ

Clear answers for the two available web apps: Shift Planner and Pricing Assistant Pro.

Shift Planner

What does Shift Planner help me do?

Shift Planner helps you build weekly employee schedules, estimate labor cost before the schedule is finalized, compare payroll against projected revenue, and check whether each day has enough coverage.

How does it calculate labor cost?

It uses each employee's wage and scheduled shift hours to calculate daily and weekly labor cost. When projected revenue is entered, it also shows labor percentage so you can spot expensive days before publishing.

Can it suggest a schedule?

Yes. Shift Planner includes a suggested schedule builder that uses employee settings, availability, roles, wages, max hours, revenue targets, and coverage needs to help create a starting point.

Can I save, export, or print schedules?

Yes. You can save schedules for later, export schedule data to CSV, and use a print-friendly schedule view for sharing with managers or staff.

How do I start using Shift Planner?

Create an account in the client portal, start the 7-day trial or subscribe, then open Shift Planner from the software dashboard.

Pricing Assistant Pro

What does Pricing Assistant Pro calculate?

It calculates a recommended price using item or service costs, labor, overhead, waste, packaging, card fees, delivery app fees, competitor pricing, and your target margin.

Is it only for restaurants?

No. It works well for restaurants and menu pricing, but it can also support service-based businesses that need to estimate cost per client visit using supplies, labor, equipment, overhead, and margin.

Can I save reusable cost templates?

Yes. You can save full cost configurations, including basic costs, advanced labor tiers, itemized overhead, delivery app fees, card fees, waste, packaging, and other pricing assumptions.

Can I save priced items into menus?

Yes. Pricing Assistant Pro lets you add priced items to saved menus, create new menus, delete saved menu items, export menu data, and return to prior pricing work later.

Does it use market signals?

Yes. Pricing Assistant Pro includes a monetary backdrop section that can use M2 money supply data as a long-range pricing pressure signal alongside your cost-based recommendation.
