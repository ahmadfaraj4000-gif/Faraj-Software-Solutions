Staff scheduling with labor cost built in

Most small and medium-sized businesses do not just need a calendar. They need to know whether the schedule makes financial sense. Shift Planner connects shifts, wages, hours, projected revenue, labor percentage targets, coverage needs, and employee limits so managers can catch expensive staffing decisions early.

Shift Planner features

Focused tools for weekly labor planning, shift management, and business cost control.

Employee setup

Add employees with names, roles, hourly wages, maximum hours, and unavailable days so the schedule reflects real staffing limits.

Labor cost calculator

See labor cost update from scheduled hours and wages, then compare the schedule against revenue expectations.

Revenue comparison

Plan with daily revenue or weekly revenue so labor percentage is tied to the way your business thinks about sales.

Coverage planning

Set coverage targets by day and avoid schedules that leave busy days thin or slow days overstaffed.

Suggested schedule builder

Generate a starting schedule using labor targets, minimum staff, busy days, availability, and max-hour rules.

Save, export, print

Save schedules, export a CSV file, print a clean schedule, and undo the last action while editing.
