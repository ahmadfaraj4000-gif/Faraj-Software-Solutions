Simple formulas behind the dashboard

The value of this page is not just the data — it's the interpretation. These formulas show how the cards turn raw numbers into owner-friendly signals.

Year-over-year change

YoY % = ((Current Value − Value 12 Months Ago) ÷ Value 12 Months Ago) × 100

Used for CPI, PPI, and M2 to show whether inflation pressure is accelerating or easing.

Price trend change

Trend Change % = ((Current Value − Prior Value) ÷ Prior Value) × 100

Used for oil to flag transport and supplier cost pressure when energy prices move sharply.

Pressure score + trend

Pressure Score = (CPI × 0.35) + (PPI × 0.25) + (Rates × 0.20) + (Oil × 0.10) + (M2 × 0.10)

The score shows current pressure after each input is normalized to a 0–100 scale. A separate trend signal then flags whether pressure is rising, cooling, or stable based on the direction of CPI, PPI, oil, rates, and M2.
