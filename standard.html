Everything Standard includes

Purpose-built for single-item costing. Simple enough to use every day, accurate enough to trust.

🧂

Ingredient Costing

Add up to 8 ingredients per item. Enter your purchase price and package size — Pricing Assistant handles unit conversions (lb → oz, kg → g, l → ml, and more) automatically.

Why it matters: Bought a 5 lb bag of flour for $3.20 but only use 4 oz per batch? Pricing Assistant converts and costs it precisely.
🗑️

Waste Tracking

Set a global waste percentage that applies to all ingredients, or override it per ingredient. Pricing Assistant folds waste into the true cost so it never gets forgotten.

Why it matters: 8% trim on chicken breast isn't just wasted food — it's lost margin on every plate you sell.
👥

Labor Cost

Enter your hourly rate and the minutes it takes to make one item. Pricing Assistant calculates the exact labor cost per plate — no guessing, no rounding.

Why it matters: 6 minutes of $15/hr labour adds $1.50 to every item. Multiply that by 200 plates a day and it adds up fast.
🏢

Overhead Allocation

Enter your total monthly overhead (rent, utilities, insurance, etc.) and how many items you sell per month. Pricing Assistant splits the overhead fairly across every item.

Why it matters: $4,000/month in overhead across 2,000 items = $2.00 per item. That's real money you need to recover.
🎯

Recommended Price

Set your target profit margin and Pricing Assistant tells you exactly what to charge. It also shows your break-even price — the minimum you can charge without losing money.

Why it matters: Stop pricing by feel. Know the floor, know the target, price with confidence.
💾

Save & Load Profiles

Save any item as a profile (.json file) and load it back any time. Perfect for building out your menu item by item, or revisiting costs when ingredient prices change.

Why it matters: Price your whole menu once, update individual items when costs change — without starting from scratch.
