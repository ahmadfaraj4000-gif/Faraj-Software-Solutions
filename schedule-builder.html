Employee Schedule Builder

Schedule Builder for Small Businesses

A schedule builder helps managers organize weekly shifts, assign employees, and plan coverage before the week starts. Shift Planner adds the business side of scheduling by connecting employee shifts to labor cost, projected revenue, and staffing coverage.

How Shift Planner helps

  • ✓Build weekly schedules for hourly employees.
  • ✓Plan shifts by employee, role, wage, and max hours.
  • ✓Compare labor cost against projected revenue.
  • ✓Save, export, and print weekly schedules.

Why this matters for small and medium-sized businesses

Instead of treating scheduling like a static calendar, Shift Planner helps small and medium-sized businesses understand whether the weekly schedule makes sense operationally and financially.
